How to play

  • Roll a 6 to bring a token out of the base onto its start square.
  • Move your token clockwise around the board by the number you rolled.
  • Land on an opponent's token to send it back to its base — but not on ★ safe squares.
  • Rolling a 6 gives you another roll. Three 6s in a row loses your turn.
  • Enter the coloured home column, then reach the centre with an exact roll.
  • Two of your own tokens can share a square (they stack).
  • First player to get all 4 tokens home wins! 🏆
